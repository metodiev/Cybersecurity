## What a capstone brief will require

A complete brief must add, at minimum:

- a bounded problem and an explicit deliverable;
- prerequisites and entry evidence;
- a threat model or research question as appropriate;
- an authorized, isolated environment and data-handling plan;
- measurable outcomes mapped to rubric criteria;
- a milestone schedule and review gates;
- required evidence, artifact packaging, and reproducibility notes;
- safety, ethics, disclosure, and cleanup requirements;
- instructor/mentor review and a defense or demo;
- explicit limitations and a definition of done.

## Why the briefs are not here yet

The current release is a **draft foundations and research-preview site**. The capstones depend on advanced modules that are still `planned`; authoring briefs before those modules would create untested promises. This is a scope decision, not evidence that the capstones have been completed.

Use [`LAB_SAFETY.md`](../LAB_SAFETY.md) and [`templates/architecture-review.md`](../authoring-templates/architecture-review.md) when a capstone is drafted. Do not invent results, target access, or completion dates.
